## The roast

> My rice cooker plays a 40-second song at 5 a.m. when the rice is done — the rice gets a standing ovation, I get woken up.

40 seconds, 5 a.m., 1 standing ovation for rice: the cooker
celebrates the rice, and the human pays with sleep.

## Why this roast works

The joke lands on recognition, not on a generic complaint.
Almost every rice cooker in an Indonesian kitchen sings when
the rice is done — a cheerful little tune, right when the
house is still asleep. The roast just points at that moment
and flips the roles: the rice is the star getting applauded,
the human is the audience who never asked for a 5 a.m. show.
Two concrete numbers (40 seconds, 5 a.m.) pin the complaint
to one person's morning instead of a vague "my gadget is
annoying", and the last three words — "I get woken up" —
are where the punch lands: the appliance celebrates, the
human pays.
